Before you revoke

Revoke is available only for retired keys. It first re-encrypts or re-authenticates every dependent record with the active replacement key. If migration fails, the key remains retired and the records are left unchanged.

What rotation means

The active key becomes retired, a new version becomes active, and historical encrypted records continue using their original version until they are migrated. Rotating CMAC_SESSION invalidates existing sessions.
